Questions, answered

Questions, answered plainly.

Five things buyers ask before they start logging deposits.

Do I have to connect my bank?
No — you can log deposits by hand, but linked accounts update your streak automatically.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Bank connections use tokenized read-only access; we never see or store your login. Your data is never sold.
What if I break my streak?
One grace week per month keeps you covered — a missed week pauses your streak instead of resetting it.
Can my partner save with me?
Yes, invite up to three co-savers to a shared goal.
Do points expire?
Points stay in your fund until you spend them on closing costs or transfer them out.

Something else on your mind? We answer in plain language, no jargon.
